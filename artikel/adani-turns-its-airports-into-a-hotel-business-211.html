As it uses its airports and surrounding land to build a hotel business, Adani is betting that its planned airport cities can attract enough businesses and visitors to support thousands of hotel rooms.

India’s biggest private airport operator is becoming a hotel developer.

Adani Group has signed deals with Indian Hotels Company and IHG Hotels & Resorts to build about 3,500 rooms across all eight of its airports, using land it already controls and leaving the hotel companies to run them.

The latest agreement, announced Oct. 29, brings together Adani Airport Holdings (AAHL) and Indian Hotels Firm (IHCL) to develop eight hotels with more than 2,000 rooms combined, across Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Lucknow and Guwahati. The hotels will operate under IHCL’s Taj, Vivanta, Gateway and Ginger brands.

The deal follows a May announcement by Adani Airport Holdings and IHG Hotels & Resorts to develop five hotels delivering nearly 1,500 rooms across Jaipur, Navi Mumbai, Mangaluru and Thiruvananthapuram. The partnership will also bring IHG’s Kimpton brand to India.
