With most gear reviews, you’re reading about that product during a snapshot in time — an editor tested a latest piece of luggage on a flight or wore a waterproof jacket on a few rainy camping trips. So when you’re someone who writes gear reviews, you have a lot of gear. Some is randomly mailed to you in the hopes you’ll like it and write about it, and there’s plenty we buy, because different types of travel call for different types of gear (and we travel whenever we can). Some gear stays in our closets, some gets donated, and some doesn’t quite live up to the hype, so we don’t write about it at all.

But with gear reviews, what readers don’t usually find out is the part that really matters the most: does it hold up? After a year or more with it, are our editors and writers still using it?

So we went back and checked what we’re still using and wearing. These are the bags we grab before flights, the clothing we’d never leave at home on a road trip, and the products we really, truly think are worth the spend. Everything below is something one of us has used not just to review, but in our daily lives and travels. In some cases, we’ve been loyal to these products for nearly a decade. For each one, you’ll see what we noted when we first tested it, and what we think now that it’s been dragged through real life. If it’s here, it’s because it’s never been relegated to the back of our gear closet.
