India’s visa changes remove one hurdle for foreign visitors. But an easier visa alone won’t bring more tourists or get India close to its target of 100 million visitors.

India has simplified its visa system, cutting the number of main categories from 13 to nine and reducing the information required on application forms by as much as 70%, as the government looks to make entry easier for foreign visitors.

The revised system should go live by the end of 2026, the country’s home ministry said.

It is the second overhaul of the visa regime in 13 months. The home ministry said it has also reduced the number of sub-categories from 49 to 23, following an earlier consolidation in October 2025.

In a recent development, the changes come as India struggles to revive inbound tourism even as the government targets 100 million visitors by 2047. The country received 9.2 million foreign tourists in 2025, down 9.4% from 2024 and about 16% below its pre-pandemic peak of 10.9 million in 2019. The overhaul addresses longstanding complaints that India’s visa process is needlessly complicated for ordinary tourists.
