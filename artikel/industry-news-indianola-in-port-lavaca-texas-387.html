Indianola, Texas a coastal town that was once the second largest port in Texas next to at the time Galveston.In 1956 the port received a shipment of camels as part of the United States Camel Corps experiment to replace horses and mules as the primary work animal for southwestern America.

Sources indicate that during the Civil War, it was occupied by the Union army, once in 1862, then again a year later. Part of that regiment later drowned moving from Indianola to Matagorda island.

The island was later hit twice again, this time by hurricanes, in 1876 and 1886. The second hurricane was accompanied by a major fire and also submerged most of the town, killing hundreds.

Now basically no sign of the town is left other than a few plaques (one being a marker of where the courthouse used to be). It is however valued as a great spot for fishing for local trout, redfish and flounder.
