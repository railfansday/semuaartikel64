📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Photo Credit: The lobby of the Marriott headquarters in Bethesda, Maryland. Marriott.

Marriott’s website and app briefly blocked loyalty member log-ins Tuesday. The firm says the outage wasn’t a cyberattack. United Airlines and many non-travel companies have suffered various tech disruptions in recent days.

Members of Marriott's loyalty program had trouble signing in Tuesday, with problems affecting access through its website and mobile app.

Outage-tracking service Downdetector first began receiving complaints about login concerns from people around 9:15 am ET, and escalated to hundreds of complaints in the following hours. The pace of complaints appeared to be declining after 1 pm.
