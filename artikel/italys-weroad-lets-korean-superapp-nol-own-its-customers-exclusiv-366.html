WeRoad is entering South Korea without selling a single trip directly, letting NOL own the customer. If it works, it could become the template for how Western tour operators get into Asia.

Adventure travel manufacturer WeRoad is entering South Korea through a partnership with travel and leisure platform NOL Universe, using the platform to reach younger travelers as it expands beyond its established European and English-speaking markets.

Industry observers note that the partnership will let South Korean travelers book WeRoad’s small-group trips through Yanolja-owned NOL’s app and website. The first seven itineraries will go live in early December, with departures starting in 2027.

Recent findings suggest that direct sales remain Italy-headquartered WeRoad’s biggest revenue channel globally, but the manufacturer is taking a different route in South Korea. It is letting NOL manage the customer relationship rather than setting up its own sales operation.

“We might leave the South Korea market covered by the partnership with NOL and never go direct,” Andrea Lamperini, chief strategy officer at WeRoad, told Skift.
