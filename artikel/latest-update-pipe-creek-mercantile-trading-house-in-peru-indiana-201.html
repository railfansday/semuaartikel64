On the east side of State Road 31 near Peru, Indiana, sits a naval gun on the grounds of the Pipe Creek Mercantile Trading House. The shop features a wide selection of items in individual booths. If you go in not knowing what you are looking for, they probably have it.

Sources indicate that the gun sits in the northwest corner of the property, along the fence line near the highway. The gun's information identifies it as a Mark 79 Mod 1 Weather Shield, which was used with the United States Navy's 3-inch/50-caliber dual-purpose anti-aircraft gun mounts. The weather shield was a lightweight combination of fiberglass and aluminum designed to protect the crew from harsh weather and heavy sea spray. The gun would have been used during World War II and afterward.

Sources indicate that typical ships carrying these guns included Dealey-class destroyer escorts, Claud Jones-class destroyer escorts, and Asheville-class gunboats. Naval experts may want to weigh in here and provide a clearer understanding of which ships used this gun.
