This privately funded, not-for-profit museum lets visitors witness the greatness and trials of the teams that made up the "Original Eight" of the Negro Leagues.

In a self guided tour explore the informative and interactive exhibits featuring the photos, artifacts and films that tell the stories of these baseball players and all the people that made these teams possible.

Sources indicate that the Exhibition Gallery Film entitled "They Were All Stars" with narration by the acclaimed James Earl Jones. This film is a museum exclusive.

A timeline of baseball and African American history along with one-of-a-kind photographs, artifacts and stories to better envision the legacies told!

The Field of Legends that allows you to step into history with a stunning recreation of a baseball diamond, featuring bronze statues of all the greatest League players in their starring positions.
