Promotions Gallery in Paris, France

🌐 Source: Atlasobscura • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (83 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Historically, a secret entrance at the School of Mines, known as the “Mines staircase,” provided students access to the Paris Catacombs.
  • Although this private access is no longer available for use due to safety reasons, the General Inspector of Quarries still grants students permission to uphold this artistic tradition.
  • Each year, on the occasion of Sainte-Barbe, celebrated on December 4th in honor of the patron saint of miners, first-year students create a painting representing their class, continuing the tradition.
