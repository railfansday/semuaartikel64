Shrine of the Most Blessed Sacrement in Hanceville, Alabama

Extraordinary architecture designed to resemble a 13th-century Italian Franciscan church, particularly the Basilica of Saint Francis of Assisi. Marble floors, gold ornamentation, stained glass, and soaring ceilings.

The Spanish religious-art workshop Talleres de Arte Granda, founded in Madrid in 1891, designed and furnished the church's interior.

Industry observers note that it was founded by Mother Angelica, the founder of EWTN, and opened in 1999. Three donors provided all of the funds needed for construction and completion.
