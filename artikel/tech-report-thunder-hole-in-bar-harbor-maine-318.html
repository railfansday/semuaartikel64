Located in Maine's Acadia National Park, along the rocky shoreline of Mount Desert Island, Thunder Hole is a natural geological formation known for producing a  booming, thunder-like sound phenomenon that gives it its name, as well as for explosive water sprays reaching up to 40 feet.

Industry observers note that resulting from glacial activity over thousands of years ago, what began as a small, natural fissure in the solid granite cliffs, slowly gave way to the 10 foot wide channel seen today. As high tide rolls in, and wave force increases, water rushes into this channel, and into a small underwater cavern contained therein.

When an incoming wave forces water into this cavern, trapped air in the cavern is compressed and then escapes, almost as if through the blow hole of a whale, with magnificent water sprays and reverberating claps that sound like thunder.

Stand in awe on this rocky outcropping, as you take in the sweeping coastal views and the natural wonders that Mother Nature has provided.

Thunder Hole is best viewed approx 2 hours before high tide (check google for tide tables).

Enter Acadia National Park and follow Park Loop Rd, which is a one way street that loops around the section of the park where Thunder Hole is located. Thunder Hole is approx 1-2 miles in from where you enter on Park Loop Road. Parking is available all along the right side of the road. You can park anywhere along here and walk the rest of the way along the pedestrian foot path that snakes along the left side of the road. There are multiple small trails along this path that lead down toward the water for closer viewing of rocky shores and beaches, etc. Take caution as some of these paths are steep and rocky. Not recommended for those with weakness or balance problems. While often steep and potentially treacherous, these little paths are short and the views are breathtaking. As you walk along,  there will be plenty of beautiful, awe inspiring sights and you may find yourself exclaiming...this must be it! Or..is this Thunder Hole? What about this spot? The signage for Thunder Hole in not great. But, rest assured, you will know it when you see it.

If a longer walk is not for you, that's ok! There is a very small gift shop and parking lot (on the right side of the street) located directly across the street from the main Thunder Hole viewing area. Parking lot does have a couple of handicapped parking spots, and the walk to the viewing area from there is quite short (approx 0.1 mile from parking lot to accessible viewing ramp and walkway). Note that parking lot fills up rapidly during peak summer months.

Alternately, you can use the free Island Explorer Shuttle bus which makes regular stops right at the entrance to the Thunder Hole parking lot.
