To save money, it may be a good idea to base your shopping proposal on maximizing discounts and minimizing the amount of cash that leaves your wallet.

So where are the best places to do this? WalletHub reviewed multiple stores and different sales to figure out which ones could offer the most out of your shopping budget. Check out these choices before you head out on Black Friday to see how much you might be able to save, based on their data.

In reviewing 11 different stores, WalletHub found that if you want the best deals for the holidays, you'll want to start at JCPenney.

According to recent reports, the retailer consistently leads this WalletHub study annually, with the best discounts since 2018. For 2026, JCPenney is expected to give shoppers an average discount of 74.33%, which is more than twice the national average of 36.39% among the full 11 retailers in WalletHub's survey. You can find substantial discounts in its stores for things like jewelry and appliances. WalletHub found that specific categories of gifts also had the best discounts, so you may want to add them to the top of your list.

Sparkly gifts may be your first stop, with jewelry leading all gift categories when it comes to the biggest discounts. On average, you can expect a 46.53% discount to pick up some shiny gifts, with JCPenney and Macy's leading the way. These stores are the only two in the study that each have an average discount above 70% for shoppers.

Check out apparel and accessories second, which are expected to have a 41.28% discount on average across the 11 stores that WalletHub reviewed. Macy's leads in this category with a 65.02% discount on average for apparel and accessories in its stores.

In addition, toys are popular with kids and popular with discounts. You can expect to find an average discount of 37.71% across stores, putting the category in third place among all options. Like apparel, Macy's leads this category with almost 60% off on average.

Industry observers note that other popular products don't have the same success when it comes to significant discounts. Computers and phones, for example, only expect a 26.17% discount, while shoppers looking for deals on consumer electronics can find an average discount of 23.59% across these 11 stores.
