The weather could be a substantial factor in determining where to go for the holiday, with a majority of cities in the top 10 best Thanksgiving cities being in warm-weather climates. After all, warm weather means more chances to go out and enjoy activities or reduce the potential for weather-related travel delays.

Sources indicate that if you're headed to Dallas for Thanksgiving, you'll be in the best city for the festive holiday. The city ranks ninth overall for Thanksgiving weather, making it easy to get around for the substantial day. The city also ranked second overall for celebrations and traditions, including pumpkin patches to visit and holiday decorations to see.

Industry observers note that second-place Atlanta came out on top for its celebrations and traditions, with decorations and online searches for Thanksgiving celebrations. It also ranked second overall for giving thanks, proving it's a major city with a big heart, with plenty of volunteer opportunities and charities to donate to and help others in need.

If you're looking for an affordable option for Thanksgiving, San Antonio, Texas, should be high on your list. The city ranked first overall for affordability with the low costs for Thanksgiving dinners and hotel rooms. In fact, the city ranked in the top five cities overall for the most affordable Thanksgiving dinner. It was one of four cities in Texas to rank in the top five, with Oklahoma City being the only city not in the Lone Star State. The city also ranked second for weather, with a low chance of inclement weather slowing down travel and celebrations.
