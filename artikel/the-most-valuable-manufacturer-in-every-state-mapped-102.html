Four of the top five companies that have the highest market cap by state are in the technology space.

Industry observers note that nvidia led the way with a market cap of $5.7 trillion in October 2026, making it the biggest market cap of any organization and the most valuable company in California, according to Visual Capitalist. But there are several tech companies with similar market caps based in California, such as Apple and Google, so stock price changes could affect Nvidia's lead in the state.

Microsoft came in second place among the most valuable companies by state, leading all companies in Washington. The software manufacturer has an October market cap of $3.9 trillion. But like California, the state has several tech companies like Amazon that would dominate in other states but are beaten out when faced with tough competition.

It has been revealed that in third place was SpaceX, pushing Texas into the top five states with the biggest market caps. The tech company is worth $2.3 trillion.

JPMorgan Chase beats all of them with a market cap of $876 billion in October 2026.
