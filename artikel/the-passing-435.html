The passing

🌐 Source: Aeon • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (110 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

The Passing captures the veterinarian Michael Mullen as he provides pet care with deep empathy, at owners’ homes in Austin, Texas. At the film’s centre is his visit with his close friend Cindy, whose beloved dog Fiona, 17, needs end-of-life care. In their portrait, the local filmmakers Ivete Lucas and Patrick Bresnan mirror Mullen’s gentle manner, documenting this process with an unobtrusive observational approach that allows the emotions at hand to unfold with a quiet subtlety and power. It’s a sombre watch, of course, but not without a certain beauty, as Fiona is given a kind and merciful death, and Cindy offers a tearful, loving goodbye.
