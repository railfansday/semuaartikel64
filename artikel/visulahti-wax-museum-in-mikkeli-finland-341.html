Rather than being an impressive, polished wax museum, the Visulahti Wax Museum has become famous for its awkward, outdated, and sometimes unintentionally hilarious figures.

According to recent reports, the museum has something of a cult reputation in Finland. Its wax figures are famously questionable in quality: many look surprisingly inaccurate, while some remind you more of a horror movie setting. In 1983, the Wax Museum was established with serious intentions, but nowadays people visit it mainly for nostalgic reasons. You have to approach the museum with a good sense of humour.

Sources indicate that the collection rarely gets updated, and the once-popular TV stars are now unfamiliar to the younger audience. The newest figurine is the Finnish president, whose resemblance – or the lack of it – has been widely discussed in the national news.

It has been revealed that there are also international stars displayed at the museum, such as Elvis, the Beatles, and Santa Claus. At the end of the tour, a creepy version of Mickey Mouse sings to you with his friends, as you walk out of the building, wondering what you just experienced.

Recent findings suggest that wax Cabinet is open only during the summer season; see the current entry price on the website. Entry is also included with the Visulahti wristband, which gives you access to other activities in the area as well.
