Wasserspeier (‘Water Spitter’) in Bern, Switzerland

Since 1984, the dragon-like Wasserspeier ("Water Spitter") on Bern’s Münstergasse has been delighting and fooling locals and tourists alike. Every spring, artist Luciano Andreani (*1947) and homeowner Tinu Mühlethaler install the spout on the roof of the historic Old Town building.

Anyone who lingers too long while bending down to pick up one of the five-franc coins scattered below is in for a surprise and gets a refreshing splash of water from above. The art installation sprays water into the street periodically. Beware - a refreshing shower is guaranteed!

The Wasserspeier is particularly popular with children and the young at heart.

The art installation is installed in spring and not available in winter.
