Slovenia’s section of the Alpe Adria Trail passes some of the country’s best cooking, from Michelin-starred dining rooms to village inns serving river trout and mountain cheese.

Slovenia’s two most decorated restaurants sit about 37 miles (60 km), or three days’ walk, apart on the trail: Milka in Kranjska Gora (two Michelin stars) and Hiša Franko in Kobarid (three). Book Milka the night before Stage 23. Hiša Franko sits in Kobarid, in the valley below Drežnica, so strategy it as a rest-day dinner or arrange a transfer through the Booking Center. Village inns fill the other nights.

Industry observers note that before or after the trail, eat in Ljubljana. The capital is a short trip from both ends of the route, and its food scene is on a roll:.
